How it works

  1. 01Create an account and open your dashboard.
  2. 02Upload a PDF, Word document, presentation or text file.
  3. 03The document is parsed and divided into referenced sections.
  4. 04Gemini builds your study package from those sections.
  5. 05Revise, practise, sit mock exams and ask the tutor questions.
What you get

Everything is generated from your document

Each resource keeps a reference back to the page, slide or section it came from, so you can check the original material whenever you need to.

Structured revision material

An overview, organised topics, detailed revision notes, a glossary and a formula reference, written for examination preparation rather than general reading.

Practice with feedback

Multiple-choice, true/false and short-answer questions with explanations. Mock examinations are timed, scored on the server and reviewed question by question.

A tutor grounded in your notes

Ask for a simpler explanation or another example. The tutor retrieves the most relevant sections of your document and says so when the material does not cover the answer.

A note on privacy and accuracy

Extracted text from your uploads is sent to Google's Gemini API to generate study material. Your documents are stored privately and are never served from a public URL. You can delete a document, and its study packages, at any time.

Generated resources are study assistance, not a replacement for your course material or your lecturer's guidance. Always check important details against the original document.
