Elasticity and market responses

Price elasticity of demand measures how strongly buyers react when a price changes. When demand is elastic, a price rise cuts the quantity sold by a larger percentage, so revenue falls. When it is inelastic, revenue rises.

If demand for a product is elastic, what usually happens to total revenue when its price rises?

  • A. It falls
  • B. It rises
  • C. It stays the same
Reveal answer

Suggested answer: A. It falls.

Buyers cut their purchases by a larger percentage than the price rose, so price multiplied by quantity goes down.

How it works

From a document to exam practice in four steps

  1. 01

    Upload your material

    PDF, DOCX, PPTX, TXT, Markdown, RTF, ODT or ODP, up to 25 MB. Text is extracted with real page and slide references.

  2. 02

    Choose how you study

    Set your academic level, exam date and how many flashcards, practice questions and exam questions you want.

  3. 03

    Work through the package

    Study the summary, topics and revision notes, then test yourself with flashcards and practice questions.

  4. 04

    Sit a timed mock exam

    Answer under exam conditions, submit, and see your score, the correct answers and the sections that need another pass.

Good to know

Generated content is assistance

AI output can be incomplete or wrong. Treat it as a study aid and check important claims against your course material and your lecturer’s guidance.

Scanned PDFs need OCR

If a PDF is only images, text extraction cannot read it. ExamPrep explains this clearly rather than producing empty study notes.

You control your data

Delete a document and its study packages, examinations and tutor conversations are removed with it. Read the privacy notice.
