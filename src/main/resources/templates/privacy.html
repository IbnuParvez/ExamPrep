Privacy

How ExamPrep handles your material

A plain-language summary of what happens to the documents you upload.

Privacy promise

Your documents stay private to your account. Extracted text is sent to Google’s Gemini API only to generate your study material or answer a tutor question. You can delete documents and study packages at any time.

What is stored

ExamPrep stores your account details (name, email, BCrypt password hash), the documents you upload, the text extracted from them, and the study packages, examinations and tutor messages generated for you. Uploaded files are kept in private storage and are never served from a public URL.

How Google Gemini is used

To generate study material, relevant extracted text from your document is sent to Google’s Gemini API, along with your study preferences. The API key stays on the server and is never exposed to your browser. Tutor questions are sent together with the most relevant document sections so answers can be grounded in your material.

ExamPrep does not send your whole document in one request when it is large: it analyses sections and combines the results. Prompts and responses are not written to application logs.

What is not done

  • Your documents are not published, shared with other students or used as public examples.
  • No advertising or analytics trackers are embedded in the application.
  • No third-party AI provider other than the configuration described above is used.
  • Fonts are served from this site, so your browser makes no third-party font requests.

Deleting your data

Deleting a document removes the stored file, its extracted text and every study package generated from it, including examinations and tutor conversations. Deleting a study package removes its flashcards, questions and attempts.

Generated content

AI-generated summaries, notes, questions and answers are educational assistance. They can be incomplete or incorrect, so verify important details against your official course material.

Quick facts

  • Documents are private to your account.
  • API keys never reach the browser.
  • Uploaded text is processed by Gemini only when you generate study material or ask the tutor.
  • You can delete documents and packages at any time.
